No quota increase was requested. Defaults already sufficient: Titan Text
Embeddings V2 is 6,000 req/min, 300,000 tokens/min (fixed); Claude Haiku
4.5 is 10,000 req/min, 5,000,000 tokens/min (adjustable). Lab 3 excludes
production-scale traffic, so real usage will be a handful of embedding
calls plus a few hundred generation calls for evaluation.

That being said, here is the math to prove it would be sufficient for if
lab 3 were to go to production: System 3's automation target (4,340
contacts/day, ~4 LLM calls each) works out to ~20-25 calls/min average or 
~75/min peak. At 30x average (~750/min, ~1.2M tokens/min), it's still well
under both ceilings. Since even full production traffic clears these
quotas, the lab's much smaller workload isn't a concern, and requesting an
increase would just pad a number with no workload behind it. The one to
watch long-term is Titan's — it's fixed, not adjustable, and System 2's
offer-generation embedding volume (~210K offers/week) is the more likely
one to approach its 300K tokens/min ceiling.
